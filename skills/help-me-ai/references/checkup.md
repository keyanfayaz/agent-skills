# Checkup

Goal: show the client what has changed since last time, find the next most valuable improvements, and refresh their path. A checkup runs when the path is finished, when the last checkup was more than about 30 days ago, or when they ask for one.

## What to read

Read only what they agreed to. If their consent is more than about 30 days old, or you want something new, ask again — specifically.

Work through, in this order, whatever applies to their tool:

1. A usage report from the last 30 days, if one exists (read it first; it saves time).
2. Personal instructions and any memory the tool keeps.
3. Settings and permission rules.
4. Personal skills, including any built in earlier sessions.
5. Connected tools.
6. A sample of history since the last checkup (or the last 60 days if this is the first).

With interview only, ask the Ask questions for each area instead, one at a time, and skip any whose answer is already in the profile.

## What to look for

- **Repeated requests or pasted blocks** of instructions — candidates for a skill.
- **Re-explaining** background, or corrections like "no, I meant…" — missing context.
- **Step-by-step steering, or tasks abandoned partway** — delegation that isn't working yet.
- **Pasted emails, documents, calendar details or data** — a connection worth making.
- **Repeated approval prompts, or approvals switched off entirely** — permissions to tune.
- **Anything that looks like a key, token or password** — a safety finding. Say where it is; never repeat it.
- **Work in their role that never shows up** — habits to grow.
- **Anything built in earlier sessions** — is it being used? A skill that never triggers, or instructions that are out of date, is a finding too.

## Levels

Compare each area with the most recent levels in the profile. Lead with what improved and the evidence for it ("you haven't re-explained your role once since we set up your instructions"). Then what stayed the same, then anything that slipped. Add a new dated levels entry; never overwrite old ones.

## Top 3 moves

Pick the three changes that will help them most for the effort, in that order. For each:

- what to change, in one sentence;
- why, with the evidence;
- roughly how long it takes;
- and end with: "I can do this now — want me to?"

If they say yes, do it now (it becomes this session's change) or, if it's bigger, make it a session on the path.

## Update the path

Add, drop or reorder path items to match what you found, and say why for each change. Keep 3–5 open items, with a checkup last. Ask them to approve, then save it to the profile.

Then wrap up as the skill's main instructions describe.
