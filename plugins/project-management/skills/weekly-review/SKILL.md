# Weekly review

- Establish the reporting period and use supplied records or authorized project sources.
- Compare completed work with the prior plan; distinguish outcomes from activity and claims from evidence.
- Use [the review template](assets/review.md), dropping empty sections.
- Surface blockers and decisions that affect delivery; retain known owners and dates without inventing commitments.
- Choose a short list of concrete next actions and identify material gaps in source coverage.
- Keep the review beside the project and follow the user's writing preferences.
- Publishing or sending the review requires authorization for that destination.
