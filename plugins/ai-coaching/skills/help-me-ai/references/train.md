# Train: a hands-on lesson

Goal: in 15–20 minutes, the client learns one new way of working with AI by doing it on their own real work. They leave able to do it without you.

## Pick the gap

Take the lesson from the path item, or from the weakest area in their latest levels. One gap per lesson. Turn it into a single skill they can practise, for example:

- **Delegation:** hand off a whole task with a clear goal, then review the result — instead of steering step by step.
- **Context:** start a session in the right folder or Project so it already knows the background.
- **Reuse:** run one of their skills, and ask for a change to it.
- **Connections:** ask a question that needs a connected app, and check where the answer came from.
- **Safety:** decide what to approve, and spot when output needs checking before it goes out.
- **Habits:** try AI on a part of their work they've never used it for.

Pick a real task from their week to practise on. Ask them for one if the evidence doesn't suggest a good candidate.

## Check current docs

Your built-in knowledge of your own tool may be out of date. Before teaching any feature of it, check the tool's current official documentation (the per-tool facts list where to look). Never send client details in a search. If you can't confirm a feature exists in their version, don't teach it.

## Run the lesson

1. **Why it matters for them** — one or two sentences, tied to their evidence ("you've steered three research tasks step by step this month").
2. **Show it** — do a short version yourself on their task, saying what you're doing and why.
3. **They do it** — on a second, real piece of their work. Coach, don't take over. If they get stuck, give one hint at a time.
4. **Recap** — what they just did, in one sentence they could repeat to a colleague.

Keep it practical. Skip theory they won't use this week.

## Wrap up

- Give them one thing to try on their own this week.
- Offer one or two further lessons that build on this one. Add any they accept to their path.
- Record what they practised in the session report and mark the path item done.
